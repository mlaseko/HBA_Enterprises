Every type is a choice under Shows when you upload a plan, pick PDF pages or tag a plan, and a sheet of that type sits under its floor's Sheet switch on the Plan page, borrowing the main plan's room boxes. The words are what the page picker looks for in a drawing title to tick the right type ("window, door": a title with WINDOW or DOORS is that sheet; a word matches the start of a word, so "electric" finds Electrical). A type can be deleted once no plan shows it; the main plan stays.
