The one-click button

Drag this link to your browser’s bookmarks bar. Then, on any website, click Save to HBA: it picks the page’s main picture and opens the form above.

Save to HBA

iPhone: Share → Save to HBA (one-time setup, about a minute)
  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, name it Save to HBA.
  2. Tap the ⓘ info button, turn on Show in Share Sheet, and under “Receive” keep URLs and Safari web pages ticked.
  3. Add the action URL and type this address, then tap Shortcut Input from the suggestions bar right after the =:
  4. Add the action Open URLs. Done.

From then on, in Safari, Pinterest or the Taobao app: tap Share → Save to HBA. The page opens here with the picture ready to save.

Safari bookmark instead (same button, no Shortcut)
  1. Bookmark any page (Share → Add Bookmark) and call it Save to HBA.
  2. Copy the code below, open Bookmarks → Edit the bookmark, paste it as the address.
  3. On a product page, open Bookmarks and tap Save to HBA.
No button? Paste a link

Tap Share → Copy link on the pin or product page, then use the Paste button above. Every item form and the Mood board page take a pasted link too.

Good to know
  • Pictures are shrunk to 1600 px and stored with the project like uploaded photos.
  • Some sites block downloads. If so, save the picture to your phone and upload it.
  • Pictures from other sites belong to their owners: fine for sample references and mood boards.
