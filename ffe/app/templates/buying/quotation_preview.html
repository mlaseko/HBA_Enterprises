What Matched, New, Merged, Extra, Skipped and % sure mean
Matched quotes a product of the design: on save it becomes that shop's quote on it.
New the design has no item for it: on save an item is created in the rooms ticked (one per room, the sheet's quantity shared) and the quote put on it. If it is really an existing product, pick it instead; if it belongs with another line, make it a part of it.
Merged a part of another line (hardware, a trap, the glass of a set): its amount joins that line's quote, no item of its own.
Extra a charge on the document (pallet, packing). Skipped nothing is saved from the line.
% sure how sure the suggestion on the line is (the product and the place): green 80 % and above, gold 45–79 % — look at it, grey below — a guess, decide yourself. The percentage on a chip under the product is how alike the words, size and rooms are. Neither says anything about the price or the quantity: the flags and the table below do.
