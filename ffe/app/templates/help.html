The idea in one minute

You enter each item once: which room it goes in, what it is and a photo of the sample. On Buying you then record what the shops quote and choose which shop supplies which rooms; the price and the brand bought go onto those rooms by themselves. From there the app gives you the client's schedule as a PDF, keeps track of what is quoted, ordered, paid, shipped and received, prints box labels by room, follows every box from the shop through the warehouse and the container to its room, and gives the client a link to follow progress. Nothing is typed twice.

Everything hangs off rooms and areas. Every item and every box carries a code such as GF-KIT, so a delivery goes straight to the place where it will be installed.

Yellow boxes are where you type. Everything else is worked out for you.

You, the designer

Log in with the app password. You do everything in this guide; the app is built for your phone in the showroom and your laptop at the desk.

Suppliers

Get a packing link, no login. They see only their items, list their own cartons and see where to deliver them.

The client

Gets a client link, no login. Read-only: photos, USD prices (once a shop is chosen), delivery status, the plan and the PDF.

The site team

Gets PDFs: the schedule without prices, room checklists with the plan, box labels, the loading list.

The walkthrough, start to finish

This is the order things happen in a real project. Each stage says what to do, where to do it, and what you have when it is done. What you enter in stage 5 is exactly what prints in stage 8.

Stage 1 · once

Set up the studio

  1. Open Studio settings (the sliders icon at the top right, or the Studio menu).
  2. Type the studio name, phone, email and website. They print on every PDF, label and share page.
  3. Set the default exchange rate (CNY per 1 USD) and upload the logo if you want it in the app. Press Save.

Stage 3

Rooms & areas

  1. Open Rooms. For each place type a code (GF-KIT), a name (Kitchen) and the floor (Ground), then press Add. Codes become upper-case and spaces turn into dashes.
  2. Leave Kind on Auto and the app decides from the name whether it is a room or an area; or choose yourself. A room is a space you furnish and finish; an area is a zone that still gets items: entrance, hall and corridors, stairs, landing, balconies, carport, roof terrace, the whole house.
  3. After an Excel import, press Sort rooms & areas by name once. The page shows a reminder until you do.

Stage 5

Items: build the schedule

There are three ways in, and you will use all three.

  1. Type it. On Items (or on a room in the plan) press Add item. Tick the rooms that get it (the chips tick all bedrooms, all bathrooms or a whole floor at once), choose the category, type the name and the must-have spec, the size and finish, the preferred brand if the design asks for one, the qty and unit, and add photos. Save, or Save & add another. No shop or price yet: those come in stage 6.
  2. Shoot it. In a showroom press Quick capture. Every photo becomes a draft at once; keep shooting. Later, on Drafts, give each one a name, room and qty and press Save as item.
  3. Save it from the web. Paste a supplier page, Taobao listing or Pinterest pin into the item's web-link field, or use the one-click Save from web button. The picture is downloaded and stored with the item.

Two rules worth knowing now: the category decides which page of the client schedule the item prints on, and the status starts at To buy and follows the purchase from there.

Every picture in the app opens on top of the page when you tap it: item photos, floor plans and drawing pages, the mood board, receipts. It arrives fitted to the window and sharp (the full-size file is fetched, not the small copy from the list); zoom with the buttons, the mouse wheel, a pinch or a double tap, drag to move, ‹ › walk through the gallery, and × or Esc, or the phone's Back button, land you back where you were. Stage 6

Buying: quotes, shops, orders, payments

The item says what is needed and where. Buying is where you decide who supplies it: it lists every product once, whatever its rooms (the same floor tile in twelve rooms is one product), with how far it has got.

  1. Quotes. In the shop, open the product on Buying and press Add a quote: the shop (from the list, or + New shop… with its name, market and WeChat; it joins the supplier book), the brand and model, the price in CNY or USD, the box size, minimum order and lead time, and a photo of the sample or the price tag. Add one quote per shop. Nothing is chosen yet, and the client sees no price.
  2. Choose. Compare the quotes: each shows what the whole order costs with its own box size, and the cheapest says Lowest price. Press Buy for the rooms without a shop on the one you choose. To split, open Choose the rooms to buy here on each quote and tick its rooms: a room is always bought whole from one shop. Those rooms' lines now carry the shop, its price, the brand bought and the lead time, and move to Quoted.
  3. Quantities. What to order adds an allowance to the rooms' quantities (10 % for tiles, nothing for the rest) and rounds up to whole boxes; change both per product. A purchase can also take a quantity set by hand.
  4. Review. Purchase schedule shows every product in the look of the client schedule, with what to order, the shop, the brand bought and the price, by category. Its PDF is the internal version, with the shops and the money by shop: filter it by market first to take one market's list with you, or print it all to review with your team.
  5. Order. What you bought from a shop waits on the Orders tab under Ready to order. Press Start an order: the shop's products go onto a numbered draft (MV-001: the project's initials and a number) with each room, what to order in whole boxes, the price and the total. Check it, set the shop's ready date and the deposit (read from the shop's payment terms), and send the shop the Purchase order PDF, or press Send on WeChat for the same order as a message with the Chinese for each heading. When the shop agrees, press Confirm the order: its rooms become Ordered by themselves.
  6. Pay. Record each deposit and balance on the order (Record a payment comes filled in with what is due) or on Payments: date, amount in CNY, type, the bank or WeChat reference and a photo of the receipt. Once an order is paid in full, its rooms become Paid. What is still to pay, per order and per shop, shows on the order, the supplier, Payments and the Overview.
  7. Track. The Tracker tab puts the money side by side (budget, estimated, committed, ordered, paid, to pay), lists what needs attention (orders past their ready date with money still to pay, deposits to pay, balances due before collection, drafts to confirm, products without a shop or a quote) and breaks the buying down by stage, shop and category.

Stage 7

Shipping: boxes, the warehouse, the container

Every box travels the same way: from the shop to the consolidation warehouse, into a container, and to site. Shipping follows each one, and the items inside follow their boxes.

  1. The shops list their boxes. Open the supplier and press Create packing link, then Copy link and send it on WeChat. With that link, and no login, the shop sees where to deliver and lists every carton: the room it goes to, what is inside, the items in it (they tick them), the size and the weight. Or add the boxes yourself on Shipping › Boxes with Add a box.
  2. Labels. The app numbers each box Box n of N for its room. Press Print box labels (six per page) and Packing list PDF; the shop has the same two buttons on its link.
  3. At the warehouse. When a shop delivers, open Shipping › Shipments and press Delivered to the warehouse on its line (or tick the boxes on Boxes and press At the warehouse). They wait under Waiting at the warehouse, which says what they fit together: LCL below about 12 CBM, else a 20ft or a 40ft container.
  4. Ship. Choose the container and press Ship them: new shipment: every waiting box goes on Shipment 1. The shipment shows the space and the weight used; write its container and seal numbers, the forwarder, the bill of lading and the dates under Details, and give the warehouse the Loading list.
  5. Loaded, sailed, arrived. Press Loaded today when the container leaves the warehouse: the items in its boxes become Shipped. Then Sailed today and Arrived today (or on another day). The Overview says when the next shipment arrives, and the Tracker warns you when one is late.

Studio settings

What it is for: what clients, suppliers and the site team see as the sender. The studio name, phone, email and website print on every PDF, on the box labels and in the header of the share pages.

  • The default exchange rate is used for new projects. Each project keeps its own rate in Project settings.
  • The logo replaces the house glyph in the app. A square PNG or JPG looks best. The PDFs keep their own layout.
  • Warehouse. The consolidation warehouse the shops deliver to: name, address and contact. Every new order's Deliver to starts from it, and it prints on the purchase order and in the WeChat message; an order can still say something else. A shop's packing link and packing list show where to deliver: its latest order's Deliver to, else this warehouse.
  • The app password, the database and the photo storage are set up on the server, not here.
  • Plan sheet types. What a floor plan can show is a list you keep here: Main plan, Furniture layout, Electrical & lighting, Plumbing & sanitary, Ceiling, Flooring & tiles and Windows & doors to begin with. Each type has a kind: a plan of the floor borrows the main plan's room boxes; a reference sheet (Windows & doors, or a door schedule you add) shows drawings of the items instead, with dots and no boxes, and its drawings can be matched to the items by reference (see The plan). Type a title, pick the kind (and the words the page picker should look for in a drawing title, such as "landscape, garden") and press Add sheet type: it is a choice under Shows on Images & plans and in the page picker at once, and a plan of that type sits under its floor's Sheet switch on the Plan page. Rename a type and every plan that shows it follows; reorder with the arrows; delete one once no plan shows it (the main plan always stays). The table Which sheet a category's items are read from (every category name your projects use) decides which sheets the schedule PDF prints before a category's table: the electrical sheets before Lighting, the windows sheet before Windows & Doors.
  • Speed. Lists, the plan dots and the PDF tables show a small copy of each photo instead of the full picture, so the Items page and the plan load several times faster. Photos taken from now on get their small copy at once; for photos from before, press Make small versions under Speed until none are left. The card also shows how many pictures the server keeps in memory for the next visitor.

Projects, Overview & import

What it is for: one project per house. It holds the rooms, items, payments, boxes, images and documents of that house. Suppliers are the only thing shared between projects.

New project and Project settings

  • Client name and project name print on the schedule cover. The delivery address prints on every box label.
  • The exchange rate (CNY per 1 USD) drives every USD figure. Changing it later does not move prices, which are stored in CNY; only the USD figures change.
  • A budget in USD shows a bar on the Overview. Status (Active, On hold, Completed) is just a label on the project card.
  • A new project starts with one entry, Whole house, for items that belong to no particular room.
  • Danger zone: deleting a project removes everything in it. You have to type delete to confirm.

The Overview

  • The tiles: items (and how many have a price), total in CNY and USD against the budget, paid and balance, items and boxes received, volume packed and the container it fits, and how many items are still to buy.
  • Item status badges: tap one to list only those items.
  • Documents: every PDF and the Excel export; see PDFs & Excel.
  • By room & area (tap a room to open it on the plan; the PDF button is its checklist), By category, By supplier with ordered, paid and balance, and the items you touched most recently.

Import from Excel

  • Press Download the template on the Import page. Its Rooms sheet already lists this project's rooms and areas, so adding a floor, a room or an area is one new row: CODE - Name, the floor, and the kind (room or area). Its Shopping List sheet takes one row per item, with dropdowns for the room, category, unit, status and optional; a room you add on the Rooms sheet appears in the room dropdown at once. A How to sheet repeats these rules.
  • Several rooms in one row. The Room dropdown starts with quick picks: All rooms, All areas, All bedrooms, All bathrooms and All on Ground floor (one per floor). You can also type several rooms with ; between them ("GF-LIV - Living room; FF-BR1 - Bedroom 1"). The row becomes one item per room, each with its own code, exactly like ticking several rooms on the item form. The category stays one per item: it decides the schedule page the item prints on.
  • Updating items in Excel. Press Export all items (also Overview › Documents › Excel export), or Export a part on the Import page to get just one category, floor, room or status; the Items page's Export button gives whatever its filter shows. It is the same file, with the chosen items filled in and each Code in the first column, so a short sheet is enough when you are fixing one category or one floor. Change prices, quantities, statuses, suppliers, specs or the room, then import the file. A row whose code matches an existing item updates that item: the cells you filled change, blank cells leave the value alone, and a single - clears a cell. The code, the photos and the dots on the plan stay; a changed room moves the item and removes its dots. Rows without a code are added as new items, so you can add and update in one file.
  • Pictures. The export shows each item's cover picture over its Photo cell. To bring pictures in, upload picture files together with the sheet (the second file box on the Import page), named after the item code ("FF-MBR-04.jpg") or the item name ("Bedside wall light.jpg"; add "-2" for a second picture of the same item), or place a picture over the row's Photo cell in Excel (Insert › Pictures › Place over Cells). A picture goes to every item the name matches, so a product in four rooms gets it four times, like the item form. Items that already have a photo keep it and the preview says so; the export's own pictures are never added again.
  • Preview first. Every upload shows a check screen before anything is saved: how many items would be added, updated, left unchanged or skipped, the new rooms and suppliers, warnings such as a room or status the app could not match, a table of the updated items with each change ("Price (CNY): 3,200 → 3,500") and a table of the new items with the codes they would get. Press Apply the import to save it, or Cancel to fix the file and upload it again.
  • The Kinondoni list format imports too. Columns are matched by their headers (Code, Room, Category, Item, Must-have spec, Brand, Size, Finish, Qty, Unit, Price (CNY), Supplier, Lead time, Status, Optional, Notes); extra columns, such as the totals in the export, are ignored.
  • Rooms are matched by code and created when new, never duplicated. A Kind column is honoured; new rooms without one are classified by name.
  • Every item row is added as a new item; items are never merged. List only what you are adding, or tick Delete all existing items in this project first to start over. Photos on deleted items are lost.
  • Rows whose Room or Item starts with (example) are ignored. Photos are not in Excel: after importing, open each item and add the sample photo, or use Quick capture.

Lists: categories, units and defaults

What it is for: the choices each project offers, set for that project alone. Open Lists in the project's menu (or the button on Project settings).

  • Categories: what the item form, Quick capture, the filters, the Excel template and Claude auto-fill offer, in the order the schedule PDFs and the lists print them. Each has an Extra to order %: the allowance Buying adds on top of the rooms' quantities (10 % for tiles to start with); a product can still set its own.
  • Units (pcs, set, m², roll…), each marked whether it is bought in whole units, so 7 pcs plus 10 % orders 8, not 7.7. The first unit is the default of a new item.
  • Payment types (Deposit, Balance…) for payments on orders and on Payments.
  • Containers, with what each takes in practice (CBM) and its payload (kg): the Shipments page suggests the smallest one the boxes fit, and each shipment's fill bars use them. LCL and Air are always offered.
  • Defaults: the deposit % when a shop's payment terms do not say, how many days ahead the Tracker warns of a ready order or an arriving shipment, how long before the ready date it asks for the balance, and the volume below which a shipment goes LCL.

Changing them

  • Type a new entry at the foot of a card and press Add. Change a name or a number and press Save: renaming a category, unit or payment type renames it on every item (or payment) of the project.
  • The arrows set the order. Containers sort themselves by size.
  • The bin deletes an entry nothing uses. One still in use asks where its items go: choose another entry and press Move and delete. That is also how to merge two categories.
  • A new project starts from the standard lists, or from a copy of another project's (the choice is on the new project form). Add what is missing at the top of Lists copies the entries another project has and this one lacks, without touching yours.
  • An Excel import that brings a category or unit the project lacks adds it to the list; the preview names it first.
  • Which plan sheets print before a category in the schedule is set for the whole studio, by category name, under Studio settings › Plan sheet types.

Rooms & areas

What it is for: the list of places in the house, each with a short code. Codes end up on items, labels and boxes, so a delivery goes straight to where it will be installed.

Rooms and areas

  • A room is a space you furnish and finish: living room, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, office. An area is a zone that still gets items but is not a room: entrance, hall and corridors, stairs, landing, balconies, verandah, terrace, carport, and the whole-house entries.
  • Both work exactly the same way: codes, items, boxes, labels, a box on the plan. The difference is only how they are listed and counted: rooms first, then areas, and "11 rooms · 8 areas" instead of "19 rooms".
  • Kind on Auto decides by the name. Sort rooms & areas by name does it for every entry at once; switch any entry back by hand.

Floors

  • The Floors card at the top of the page is the list every Floor dropdown offers: on a room, on a plan, in the import. Add a floor there (Add floor), or pick + New floor… in any Floor dropdown and type the name: it joins the list.
  • Each floor is a floor of the house (printed "Ground floor") or a separate area (the staff quarters, a guest house, the garden: printed with its own name, with its own plan and its own section in the schedule by floor). The kind is guessed from the name; change it in the list.
  • Rename a floor and every room and plan on it follows. Order with the arrows: the Rooms page, the plan tabs and the schedule by floor use that order. Merge into moves everything from one floor onto another and drops the first one: the way to fix a typo ("Groud") or to fold Roof into Second. Delete works only on an empty floor.
  • "Whole house", "Site" and "Outside" are not floors: entries with those are listed under Whole house / other. A floor typed with a different spelling ("ground floor") is stored as the list spells it ("Ground").

Adding and editing

  • Code: short and unique, upper-case, dashes instead of spaces. GF for ground floor, FF for first floor is a good habit (GF-KIT, FF-MBR). Codes are never renumbered, because they are printed on labels and purchase orders.
  • Floor: pick it from the list, so the plan and the rooms find each other. The floor list above is where floors are added, renamed, reordered or merged.
  • Floor area and wall tile area in m² are optional and help you work out tile quantities. Notes are for the installers (finish level, access).
  • Open an entry to edit it: change the code, name, floor, kind or order and press Save. From there: Items, Checklist PDF, Labels, On the plan.
  • Inspiration. Each entry carries a strip of mood-board pictures for that room: what the client likes, in their words. Add yours with a photo or a web link and a caption; the client adds theirs through their link (marked "client"). The same strip shows on the room's panel on the plan and on its item list, so the taste sits next to what is being bought. The Images & plans page lists them under the mood board, whole house first, then room by room, and a picture can be moved to another room there. A star on one of a room's pictures makes it the room's cover: a thumbnail on the room's card here and a strip when the card is open, a banner at the top of its panel on the plan and a thumbnail in the plan's room list, for you and for the client. Without a star the newest picture stands in, yours before the client's; press the star again and the newest stands in once more.
  • Deleting an entry keeps its items and boxes; they lose their room and show under "Not in a room or area" until you file them again. Its inspiration pictures stay, as whole-house ones.

Floor plans & PDF pages

What it is for: the architect's drawings, kept with the project. They print in the client schedule and the room checklists, and they power the interactive plan. The same page holds the cover picture and the mood board of the client schedule.

Adding plans

  • JPG or PNG pictures are added straight away; give the floor, and optionally the sheet reference and a caption, before you upload.
  • A PDF drawing set goes through the page picker (next). You can upload a revised set later: pages already added stay even if you delete the old PDF.
  • Plans are kept at a higher resolution than photos so room names stay readable when you zoom.
  • Drawings are a reference only: rooms and items are never created from them. You decide the scope.

The page picker

  • At upload, the app reads the title block of every page: the drawing title ("Ground Floor Plan (Furniture Layout)"), the sheet number after "Drawing No" (A-101) and the floor the title names.
  • Pages titled floor / roof / site plan are ticked, with floor, sheet and caption filled in. Elevations, sections, services drawings, notes that refer to a plan and cover sheets with a drawing list are left unticked.
  • Main plan or layer? Every ticked page is a plan sheet; the Shows box under it decides what kind. Leave "Main plan (general floor plan)" for the floor's general plan. For an electrical, plumbing, ceiling, flooring or furniture sheet, give the page the same floor as that main plan and choose its layer under Shows: it lands under the floor's Sheet switch on the Plan page and borrows the room boxes. The reader prefills Shows from the title ("Electrical Layout Plan" → Electrical & lighting); a sheet titled only "Ground Floor Plan" is offered as the main plan, so change Shows yourself when such a sheet is really a layer.
  • Check, correct, untick, and press Add ticked pages. Nothing is added until you do.
  • A scanned PDF has no text to read, so nothing is prefilled: tick the plan pages and type the floor yourself.

Tagging, and several sheets for one floor

  • Every plan carries a floor, a sheet reference, a caption and what it shows; change them under the picture and press Save. The caption prints with the plan ("Ground floor · A-101 · Furniture Layout").
  • Which plan is the main plan? The sheet set to Main plan (general floor plan): the architect's floor plan of that floor, with or without furniture drawn on it. Under every plan the page says Main plan of its floor or Layer of it. With two general plans on one floor (a dimension plan and a setting-out plan, say), the one that carries room boxes is the main plan. A floor that only has a furniture layout uses it as the main plan until a general plan arrives.
  • Plans from before layers existed were filed as separate floors ("Ground Electrical"). Such a plan shows a yellow note: press Make it a layer of Ground floor and it moves under that floor as its electrical sheet, keeping any boxes it already has. Or set the floor and Shows by hand and press Save.
  • What a sheet shows: Main plan, Furniture layout, Electrical & lighting, Plumbing & sanitary, Ceiling, Flooring & tiles, Windows & doors (a reference sheet: drawings of the items, no room boxes), or any sheet type you add under Studio settings. The main plan is the general floor plan of the floor: it carries the room boxes, prints in the schedule overview and is where rooms are marked. Every other sheet of the same floor is a layer of it: it appears under a Sheet switch on the Plan page, borrows the room boxes, and holds its own item dots (sofas on the furniture layout, pendants on the electrical sheet). The page picker reads this from the title block ("Ground Floor Plan (Furniture Layout)" becomes the furniture layer of Ground floor, "Electrical Layout Plan" its electrical layer).
  • A plan without a floor prints up front in the schedule and does not join a floor's tab on the Plan page.
  • "n rooms marked · mark rooms" under a plan jumps to mark mode for that plan.

What it is for: browsing and updating the house room by room, straight on the drawing. Tap a room and its items appear; tap a dot and you see which item sits there. It is also what the client sees on their link.

Buying: quotes & shops

What it is for: deciding, product by product, which shop supplies which rooms and at what price, on the spot in the market, and seeing at a glance what is still to source.

Its tabs: Products (the list below), Orders and the Tracker (see Orders & payments), and the Purchase schedule.

The list

  • One card per product: the room lines with the same name, size and finish, whatever the rooms. Each card shows the photo, the rooms and floors, what to order, the price (the committed total once bought, the best quote before), the shop, and a bar of the rooms that have their shop.
  • The tiles under the totals are the stages, and each one is a filter: To source (no quote yet), Quoting (quotes in, no shop chosen), Part sourced (a shop chosen for some rooms), Sourced (every room has its shop and price), Ordered (every room on a confirmed order), Paid (every room's order paid in full). Filter by category or floor too, and the search box searches as you type: product, code, room, floor, size, finish, shop, brand.
  • The totals: products, room lines with a shop, quotes recorded, and Committed: what the chosen shops cost, allowances and whole boxes included.

A product

  • The design asks for: the photo, size, finish, the preferred brand and the must-haves, as the item says. Open the item to change them.
  • What to order: what the rooms need, plus the allowance (each category's Extra to order under Lists: 10 % for tiles to start with, nothing for the rest), rounded up to whole boxes once the box size is known. Allowance, pack and buying notes changes them for this product; an empty allowance goes back to the default.
  • Quotes: one card per shop with its price per unit (and in USD), brand and model, box size, minimum order, lead time, photo and notes, and what all the rooms would cost at that quote with its own box size. The cheapest says Lowest price; the ones you buy from say Buying for n rooms. Edit the quote corrects it (the rooms bought from it follow) or deletes it (what was bought from it stays bought).
  • Buy: Buy for the rooms without a shop, or Choose the rooms to buy here to tick them; ticking a room another shop supplies moves it. A room is bought whole from one shop, never half.
  • Buying from: each chosen shop with its rooms, what to order for them and the total, and the order it is on (or Start an order / Add to draft). Rooms changes its rooms, Quantity & notes sets the order quantity by hand, Stop buying here leaves its rooms without a shop again (the quote stays, and the product leaves its order).
  • Rooms: every line of the product by floor, with its status, shop and price.

The Purchase schedule

Every product by category, in the look of the client schedule: photo, codes, product and must-haves, rooms, what to order, the shop and the brand bought, the unit price, the total and the stage. Filter by category, floor or stage, show the prices in CNY or USD, and press Print / PDF to save it.

An item renamed with Apply to the other rooms keeps its quotes and purchases; a line renamed on its own becomes another product and leaves its shop. Deleting a supplier removes its quotes and purchases, and the rooms it supplied have no shop again.

Under it, After posting, across all shops counts the products that would have at least one quote from any shop, and Still to source lists, by category, the products that would still have none. It never posts anything, so a draft can be weighed before it is saved.
  • Quantities against the design, in the same card, checks every product the sheet quotes against the full list: the sheet's total quantity (all its lines on that product added up), what the rooms need (plus the allowance for cuts where the category has one: tiles, not windows), and the gap. A product whose lines carry the same supplier item number as another product's gets a note: for a running length against an area (the skirting cut from the floor slab) with the m² it amounts to, to check against the floor's surplus; for the same model on two products in the same unit (a room's own air conditioner and the general one) a reminder that this is right only when the design meant it. Claude is told the same rule and matches such lines by their place, not by the number. Countable units compare as one (a supplier's set of a mirror is the design's pcs; only m², m and the like differ). A line short of the need by more than a tenth is red; a quantity well above it is gold (another room the sheet includes, or spare); a product whose lines carry several supplier item numbers is red (two different tiles would be averaged into one price), and lines priced differently are gold. A line quoted in another unit than the product's is counted apart. Fix a wrong match on the line itself and the check follows.
  • Split by model. When several lines with different models or prices land on one product (a generic air conditioner in ten rooms quoted as four Gree models at four prices), a card above the simulation offers Split … by model. Unsplit, the lines become one quote at an average price. Ticked, each line shows its model (read from the sheet: the capacity when every line names one — 1 HP, 1.5 HP; a window's own size when the sizes differ; the place when the places differ — bath floor, bath wall; the words of the name that differ; else the supplier's code; editable) grouped by model (each model's lines under its heading), and the rooms it is for (from its place on the sheet: a room named in full wins, a one-letter misspelling still matches; × takes a room off, + room adds one), and on save every model becomes a product of its own — Air conditioner · 1.5 HP — in those rooms, with its own quote, so Compare shops and Buy work per model. Rooms no line names stay on the product as it was; a room on two models gets a second line. The table then shows the family as one item with its variants under it.
  • Save as draft keeps the preview as it stands, every choice and the document fields, without posting anything. It waits under Drafts and uploads under way on the Quotations page; Continue reopens it as you left it, Discard drops it. A sheet still being read, or one that failed, shows there too.
  • Save the quotation: nothing is saved before, and the items of the design are never changed by a sheet (the flags show where the offer differs). A shop that already quoted a product has that quote corrected, with the rooms bought from it following, never a second quote; the note under each product pick says whether the line adds or updates one. Lines matched to the same product become one quote (the total amount over the total quantity, so four windows of one size at four prices give one price per window); a line merged into another adds its amount; the discount is applied pro rata; the supplier's picture and item number go on the quote; the lead time comes from the sheet. The matches are remembered for this shop's next sheet, and the wording for every shop: a line another supplier writes the same way is matched at once. A supplier's item number that covered several products (one slab cut into floor, skirting and steps) is trusted on its own only when the wording agrees too; otherwise the products it meant are offered as chips.
  • The page, top to bottom: the counts with Show chips (All · To check · New · Merged · Skipped) and Next to check, then the lines, then If you posted it now, then The document folded with the shop and the total on its summary (open it to correct the date, validity, discount or terms), then the save bar with n to decide, which jumps to the next line that still needs you (on a phone it floats above the tab bar). Forgetting the shop no longer loses your choices: the preview comes back with them and The document open.
  • A line the matcher is not sure of (under 45 %) is left without a product rather than pre-filled with a guess; the chips and the search box are there, and Before you save lists it. When you pick another product than the one suggested, the size, unit and quantity flags are recomputed for your pick and the % sure pill steps aside.
  • Warnings above the lines: no currency on the sheet, and the lines not adding up to the total less the charges (a summary table read as lines, or a line missed). Deleting a quotation keeps the quotes a purchase uses and those a later sheet from the same shop corrected. A proforma with a summary table and its breakdown (Kitchen 8,900, then cabinets, countertop, sink… adding up to 8,900) is read as one: the parts come pre-set as Part of the summary line and their amounts count once (the quote's note says "Made up of"). On any merged line an Also L3–L11 as parts of L2 chip puts the rest of the section under the same line in one press. A revised sheet from the same shop (saved with Upload a revised sheet on the earlier document's page, which pre-selects the shop) corrects the quotes and is marked revision on the list; the earlier document moves under Earlier revisions and the two link to each other. A quotation past its validity shows expired on the list, its page and the product page: ask the shop to confirm before ordering.

The document and the inquiry sheet

  • A quotation's page has a Products and variants card: what the sheet priced, product by product, with the variants of a product split by model (its sizes), the shop's model and price for each, the lines behind it and its rooms. Rename a variant, or move a room line to another variant with the dropdown next to it, and Save the variants: the room lines take the new size, the products on the Buying page follow with their quotes and purchases, and two variants given the same name become one product. The names here are what the next sheet is matched against.
  • Each quotation keeps every line as written and what it became, the terms, the original file, and a Not quoted list: the products of the same categories the shop did not price. The quote on the Buying page names the quotation it came from.
  • Inquiry sheet for a supplier: one row per product of a category or a floor, with our code, the photo, the spec, the rooms and the quantity, and yellow cells for their item number, price, pack and lead time. Send it instead of a free-form list: a reply that keeps the code column matches every line by itself.
  • Deleting a quotation deletes the quotes it made that nothing was bought from; rooms already bought from one keep their price.
  • Compare shops (button on the Quotations page and on a document): the products of one category against every shop that priced them, a column per shop, the lowest price per unit in green, bought here on the shop the rooms are on, a product with variants shown as a family row ("all 4 priced") with its variants under it, and a Buy button that buys the product from that shop for the rooms without a shop yet, in one press (the product page can still split rooms between shops). The shop's header counts what it priced and adds it up for the rooms' quantities; the tile "every product at its lowest price" is the floor, not a plan: one shop for a whole category usually ships better than the cheapest cell of each row.
  • On the preview, Skip the lines without a product sets every unmatched line to Skip at once and Skip this section on a section heading skips all its lines (a basement, a building out of scope). An upload the server lost (a restart while reading; "Stuck" after ten minutes of silence) is marked failed and can be discarded; an old .xls file says on the preview that its pictures could not be read.

Prices & currency

Prices are kept in CNY, because that is what you pay. Every total, PDF and export uses the CNY price, and the USD figures come from the project's exchange rate.

  1. A price is typed only on a quote, on the Buying page. Choose CNY or USD next to it; the line below shows the conversion as you type. A price typed in USD is kept in CNY at the project's rate, and the quote remembers it was typed in USD.
  2. An item has no price until a shop is chosen for its room: then the room's line takes the shop's price. Correct the quote and every room bought from it follows.
  3. The currency you pick is remembered on your phone for the next quote.

To change the exchange rate, open Project settings. Prices already stored in CNY do not move; only the USD figures change. The client always sees USD, and sees no price for a room until a shop is chosen for it.

Quick capture & drafts

What it is for: shooting samples fast, without typing. Each photo becomes a draft you complete later, with good Wi-Fi.

  1. Press Quick capture (the camera button, also in the middle of the bottom bar on a phone). Choose the room and category if you know them, then Take photo. Every photo is saved straight away as a draft and the counter goes up.
  2. Later, open Drafts. Give each draft a name, room and quantity, and press Save as item. It gets a code and starts counting. All fields… opens the full form. A price you were given goes on the item's Buying page as a quote from that shop.

Drafts never appear in totals, PDFs, Excel or the client link until they have a name. The Drafts badge in the menu shows how many are waiting.

Orders & payments

What it is for: turning what you bought into numbered orders the shops confirm, paying them, and knowing at any moment what you owe each shop and what is late.

The cycle

  1. To buy: the item is on the schedule, no shop chosen yet.
  2. Quoted: a shop is chosen for the room on Buying, at its price. Its product waits on Orders under Ready to order, and stays Quoted on a draft order.
  3. Ordered: its order is confirmed with the shop.
  4. Paid: its order is paid in full.

The last two follow the orders by themselves: confirming an order makes its rooms Ordered, paying it in full makes them Paid, and Back to a draft, taking a product off or deleting a confirmed order puts them back to Quoted. A draft changes nothing but To buy, so a status you set before the order stays until you confirm it. On a confirmed order, To buy, Quoted, Ordered or Paid set by hand goes back with the order's next change. Shipped and Received follow the boxes holding the item (see Boxes), and a line on no order and in no box keeps whatever status you give it.

Orders

  • An order is what you buy from one shop at one time, numbered per project: MV-001, MV-002… (the project's initials). Orders, a tab of Buying, shows what is ready to order per shop, then the orders by stage: Draft (not confirmed yet), Deposit due, Balance due and Paid.
  • Start an order puts everything waiting from that shop on a draft. What you buy from the shop later joins it with Add to draft (here, on the product page or the supplier's page) or Also bought from… on the order. The × takes a product off: its rooms keep the shop and wait for another order.
  • Details: the shop's ready date, the deposit % (read from the payment terms, 30 % when they say nothing), the shop's own order number, the payment terms, Deliver to (every new order starts with the warehouse from Studio settings) and notes for the purchase order.
  • Confirm the order once the shop agrees, with the date and the ready date: its rooms become Ordered. Back to a draft undoes it. If a product or a price changes after that, the order says Changed since it was confirmed with both totals: send the shop the new purchase order and, once they agree, press The shop agreed.
  • The quantities are what to order: the rooms' quantities plus the allowance, in whole boxes, as set on each product's Buying page. The order follows them.
  • Delete order keeps the products bought (they wait for another order, their rooms back to Quoted) and the payments (they stay with the shop).

The purchase order and WeChat

  • Purchase order is the PDF to send: your studio, the order number and date, the shop, where to deliver and the project; each product with its photo, size, finish and brand, the rooms it is for with their codes, the quantity in boxes, the price and the amount; total, deposit, paid and balance; the payment terms, the notes, the packing instruction and a line for the shop to sign. A draft prints as a draft.
  • Send on WeChat opens the same order as a message with the Chinese for each heading (采购订单, 定金, 尾款, 交货日期…): Copy the message and paste it in the chat. On a phone, Share the PDF sends the purchase order to WeChat directly.

Payments

  • On the order, Record a payment comes filled in with what is due: the deposit first, then the balance. Add the date, the bank or WeChat reference and a photo of the receipt. The bin removes a payment recorded wrongly.
  • On Payments, choose the order a payment is for (or none: a payment to the shop in general). The tiles show what is on confirmed orders, paid, and still to pay; By supplier shows what was bought, on orders, paid and to pay; every payment shows its order.
  • Paid the shop before its order existed (or to the shop in general)? Those payments show on the order under Paid to … before, on no order: tick the ones that paid for it and press Count them toward the order.
  • To pay = the confirmed orders minus what was paid. A draft counts once it is confirmed.

The Tracker

  • The money in one row: Budget (Project settings, in CNY at the project's rate), Estimated (the shops chosen, and the lowest quote for the rest), Committed (bought from shops), Ordered, Paid and To pay, with a bar against the budget.
  • Needs attention, most pressing first, one line per order: past its ready date with money still to pay, changed after it was confirmed, a deposit to pay, the balance due before collection (ready within two weeks), ready this week, paid and waiting to be collected; then drafts to confirm, what is bought but on no order, and products with rooms without a shop, quoted but not bought, or with no quote. Tap a line to deal with it.
  • By stage (tap one to list its products), By shop (bought, ordered, paid, to pay and the next ready date) and By category (estimated, committed, ordered and paid).

Boxes & labels

What it is for: making sure every box reaches the right room and nothing is missing on site. Shipping opens on Boxes, one line per box; Shipments is the tab beside it.

  • Where each box is. The four tiles count the boxes at the shop (listed, not delivered yet), at the warehouse (waiting for a shipment), on a shipment and received on site; press one to list only those. The Where column says the same per box, with its shipment, the stage and when it arrives.
  • Add a box: the shop, the room it is installed in, what is inside, the items inside (tick them: the list shows the room's lines, only that shop's once you choose one), other codes or notes, the quantity, the size in cm, the weight and notes; tick Already at the warehouse if it is there. Shipments

What it is for: getting the boxes from the consolidation warehouse to site, container by container, and knowing at any moment what is where and when it arrives.

The Shipments page

  • Waiting at the warehouse: the boxes the shops delivered that are on no shipment yet, by shop, with their volume and weight and what they fit together (LCL below about 12 CBM, sharing a container, a point you can change under Lists; else a 20ft, a 40ft or a 40ft high cube). Ship them: new shipment puts them all on a new shipment in the container chosen beside it; Add all to Shipment n adds them to one being planned.
  • Still at the shops: boxes listed but not delivered yet. Delivered to the warehouse marks one shop's boxes at once.
  • Then the shipments by stage: Planning (gathering boxes), Loaded (left the warehouse), At sea, Arrived (at the port) and Delivered (on site), each with its container, its boxes, how full it is and when it arrives.
  • Shipments are numbered per project (Shipment 1, Shipment 2…), and a number is never given twice.

A shipment

  • The steps along the top: Gathered, Loaded, Sailed, Arrived and Delivered. The button under them takes the next step today, or on the day you pick beside it; Undo the last step takes it back.
  • On board: the boxes, the volume and the weight, with bars against what the container takes (about 28 CBM in a 20ft, 58 in a 40ft, 68 in a 40ft high cube) and a red note when it is too full.
  • Boxes on board, by the room they go to. Until it sails, the × takes a box off (it waits at the warehouse again) and Add boxes lists the ones waiting; Also plan with boxes still at the shops adds those not delivered yet.
  • Details: the container or mode, the container and seal numbers, the bill of lading (or air waybill), the forwarder, the vessel, the ports, the dates, the freight and charges in CNY and notes. With an ETA, the Tracker tells you when it is due and when it is late.
  • Receiving: once it has arrived, tap the square of each box as it is checked on site, Room received for a room, or All received. When every box is received, the shipment is Delivered by itself.
  • Loading list is the PDF for the warehouse and the forwarder: the container, seal, bill of lading, forwarder, vessel, ports and dates, then every box by room with its label, shop, contents, items, size, volume and weight, and a square to tick at loading and one on site.
  • Delete shipment keeps the boxes: they wait at the warehouse again, and their items are no longer Shipped.

The Tracker's Needs attention lists a shipment that is late or arrives within a week, boxes still to receive on site, a planned shipment that should have sailed, and boxes waiting at the warehouse; its Shipping section counts the boxes by place and lists the shipments. The Overview says when the next shipment arrives.

Questions & problems

The app feels slow.

Three different things look the same. The first page after a quiet spell takes a few seconds and the next ones are quick: Replit starts the app and the database wakes up, which is normal; an always-on deployment removes it. Pages with many photos slow on a device that has not seen them yet: open Studio settings and press Make small versions until no older photo is left, so lists load small copies. Every page slow, even without photos: the database is far from the app or asleep; tell your developer.

The electrical plan shows no room boxes.

Boxes are drawn on the floor's main plan and borrowed by the other sheets of that floor. Check that both sheets carry the same floor name, and that the general floor plan is set to show "Main plan (general floor plan)" under Images & plans. An electrical sheet uploaded before layers existed usually sits under its own floor name ("Ground Electrical"): the Images & plans page shows a yellow note on it with a one-press fix. If the electrical sheet is framed differently and the borrowed boxes sit off, press Mark rooms on it and copy the boxes to adjust them there.

Can I change many statuses or quantities at once?

Yes, in Excel. Import → Export all items (or Export a part: a category, floor, room or status; or the Items page's Export of what its filter shows), change the cells, import the file back. Rows are matched by their Code, so the items are updated rather than added again; blank cells leave a value alone and - clears one. Photos and plan dots stay. Price, Supplier and Lead time are grey: they come from Buying, and the import leaves them alone.

Can I add my own categories or units?

Yes, per project: open Lists in the project's menu. Add, rename, reorder or delete categories, units, payment types and containers there. Renaming one renames it on every item of the project; deleting one still in use asks where its items go. A new project can start from a copy of another project's lists.

Where did the supplier and price fields of the item go?

To Buying. The item now says only what is needed and where; the shop, its price, the brand bought and the lead time are chosen per product from the quotes, for whole rooms, and then show on the item, the lists, the documents and the client link as before.

I set a room to Paid and it went back to Ordered.

A room line on an order takes its status from the order: Ordered once the order is confirmed, Paid once it is paid in full. Record the payment against the order (on the order, or on Payments with the order chosen) and its rooms become Paid. Shipped and Received follow the boxes holding the item.

An item stays Ordered although its box was loaded.

The app knows what is in a box from the items ticked on it, or their codes typed in it. Open the box (Shipping › Boxes, the pencil) and tick the item: it becomes Shipped once every box holding it is loaded. An item spread over several boxes waits for the last one.

The shop delivered, but its boxes are not on the Shipments page.

Boxes show up once they are listed. Ask the shop to list them on its packing link, or add them on Shipping › Boxes with Already at the warehouse ticked. Listed boxes not delivered yet are under Still at the shops: press Delivered to the warehouse.

A box went on the wrong shipment.

Until the shipment sails, open it and press × on the box: it waits at the warehouse again, ready for another shipment. On Shipping › Boxes, tick it and choose the right shipment beside Put on.

I paid the shop before I made the order.

Open the order: the payments to that shop recorded without an order are listed under Paid to … before, on no order. Tick the ones that paid for this order and press Count them toward it; the order's paid total, its stage and its rooms follow. A status you set by hand before the order stays while the order is a draft.

The shop changed a price after we confirmed the order.

Correct the quote on the product's Buying page: its rooms and the order follow, and the order says Changed since it was confirmed with both totals (the Tracker lists it too). Send the shop the new purchase order; once they agree, press The shop agreed on the order.

We buy the same tile from two shops.

Open the product on Buying and add both quotes. On each quote open Choose the rooms to buy here and tick its rooms: a room is bought whole from one shop. Each shop then has its own line under Buying from, and the Purchase schedule shows both with their rooms.

I want one line per item, not one per room.

Open Items: it opens on the Item list (the view switch at the right of the filters brings it back). The same product (same name, size and colour/finish) in several rooms is one line there, with its rooms, the total quantity and value and a count per status; a different size or colour is another line. Press a column heading to sort; the Table view sorts the same way.

How should I name items so the list stays short?

Keep the name to what the thing is (“Floor tiles”, “Wall tiles”, “Pendant light”) and put the variation in Size (“1200 x 600 mm”) and Colour / finish (“Ceramic, matt, cream”). The app treats name + size + finish as one product: tiles of one size and colour across twelve rooms are one line in the Item list and one product on Buying, and a change applied to the other rooms only reaches those twelve. A different size or colour is its own product; a preferred brand does not make another one. The fields suggest the values already used, so pick from the list rather than retyping.

I uploaded the plan, but the room is not listed under it on the Plan page.

The room's floor and the plan's floor must have the same name. Check the floor on the Rooms page and under the plan on Images & plans ("Ground" and "Ground floor" count as the same; "GF" does not). In mark mode, rooms of other floors are in the folded list below, so you can still place them.

The PDF pages were not ticked in the page picker.

The PDF has no readable text (a scan or a flattened export), so there was nothing to read. Tick the plan pages and type the floor and sheet yourself. Everything else works the same.

A photo upload failed.

Usually the Wi-Fi. Take the photo again; Quick capture keeps the ones that went through. Photos are shrunk before upload, so one photo at a time nearly always goes through even on a slow connection.

An item is in the wrong room.

Open the item and change Room or area, then save. Its code stays as it is (codes are never renumbered); its dot on the plan is removed so you can place it again in the new room.

I deleted a room by mistake.

Its items and boxes are kept; they show under "Not in a room or area" on the Overview and as "Unassigned" in the item filter. Add the room again and set it on each item.

Why can the client see prices?

The client link shows USD prices, because the schedule is a priced document for the client; a room shows its price only once a shop is chosen for it. For the site team or a supplier, use Schedule without prices or Site schedule by floor under Documents.

The USD totals changed although I changed no price.

The project's exchange rate changed (Project settings). Prices are stored in CNY and do not move; the USD figures follow the rate.

Why is the code of a deleted item not reused?

Codes are printed on labels, purchase orders and packing lists that may already be with a supplier. A code never changes meaning once it exists.

A supplier cannot open their packing link.

Check the whole link was copied (press Copy link again). No login is needed. If the link was disabled, press Create packing link again and send the new one.

Can two people work at the same time?

Yes. There is one password and one set of data; each page shows the latest state when it loads.

A photo is missing from an item.

Open the item and upload it again. Photos are kept in the project's storage, never on your phone only.

I want to start a project over.

To replace the items, use Import with Delete all existing items in this project first ticked. To remove the whole project, open Project settings and use the danger zone. Both cannot be undone.
