The idea in one minute

You enter each item once: which room it goes in, what it is, a photo of the sample, the supplier and the price. From that one entry the app gives you the client's schedule as a PDF, keeps track of what is quoted, ordered, paid, shipped and received, prints box labels by room, and gives the client a link to follow progress. Nothing is typed twice.

Everything hangs off rooms and areas. Every item and every box carries a code such as GF-KIT, so a delivery goes straight to the place where it will be installed.

Yellow boxes are where you type. Everything else is worked out for you.

You, the designer

Log in with the app password. You do everything in this guide; the app is built for your phone in the showroom and your laptop at the desk.

Suppliers

Get a packing link, no login. They see only their items and list their own cartons.

The client

Gets a client link, no login. Read-only: photos, USD prices, delivery status, the plan and the PDF.

The site team

Gets PDFs: the schedule without prices, room checklists with the plan, box labels.

The walkthrough, start to finish

This is the order things happen in a real project. Each stage says what to do, where to do it, and what you have when it is done. What you enter in stage 5 is exactly what prints in stage 8.

Stage 1 · once

Set up the studio

  1. Open Studio settings (the sliders icon at the top right, or the Studio menu).
  2. Type the studio name, phone, email and website. They print on every PDF, label and share page.
  3. Set the default exchange rate (CNY per 1 USD) and upload the logo if you want it in the app. Press Save.

Stage 2

Create the project

  1. On Projects, press New project. Type the client's name, the project name (the house), the delivery address, the exchange rate and, if you like, a budget in USD. Press Create project.
  2. You land on the project's Overview. The sidebar (or the More menu on a phone) now shows this project's pages.
  3. Already have the list in Excel? Open Import, press Download the template, fill its Rooms and Shopping List sheets, choose the file and press Import. Rooms and items are created in one go. Otherwise carry on to stage 3 and add the rooms yourself.

Stage 3

Rooms & areas

  1. Open Rooms. For each place type a code (GF-KIT), a name (Kitchen) and the floor (Ground), then press Add. Codes become upper-case and spaces turn into dashes.
  2. Leave Kind on Auto and the app decides from the name whether it is a room or an area; or choose yourself. A room is a space you furnish and finish; an area is a zone that still gets items: entrance, hall and corridors, stairs, landing, balconies, carport, roof terrace, the whole house.
  3. After an Excel import, press Sort rooms & areas by name once. The page shows a reminder until you do.
{{ shot('rooms', 'Rooms & areas, grouped by floor. The heading tells the truth about the house: here 11 rooms and 8 areas.') }}
Done when: every place has a code, a floor and a kind. Items, boxes and labels can now be filed under them.
Stage 4

Floor plans and the interactive plan

  1. Open Images & plans. Under Add floor plans, choose the architect's PDF (or JPG / PNG pictures of the plans) and press Upload.
  2. For a PDF, the page picker opens. The app has read the title block of every page: the pages titled floor plan, roof plan or site plan are ticked, with floor, sheet number and caption filled in. Check them, fix what is wrong, untick elevations or sections if any slipped through, then press Add ticked pages.
  3. Open Plan and press Mark rooms on the main plan. Pick a room in the list, drag a box over it on the drawing. It saves at once and the list moves on to the next room. A rough box is enough. Press Done marking when the list is green. The furniture layout, electrical, plumbing and other sheets of the same floor borrow these boxes, so they are drawn once.

Stage 5

Items: build the schedule

There are three ways in, and you will use all three.

  1. Type it. On Items (or on a room in the plan) press Add item. Tick the rooms that get it (the chips tick all bedrooms, all bathrooms or a whole floor at once), choose the category, type the name and the must-have spec, qty, unit and price (CNY or USD), pick the supplier if you know it, add photos. Save, or Save & add another.
  2. Shoot it. In a showroom press Quick capture. Every photo becomes a draft at once; keep shooting. Later, on Drafts, give each one a name, room, qty and price and press Save as item.
  3. Save it from the web. Paste a supplier page, Taobao listing or Pinterest pin into the item's web-link field, or use the one-click Save from web button. The picture is downloaded and stored with the item.

Two rules worth knowing now: the category decides which page of the client schedule the item prints on, and the status starts at To buy and follows the purchase from there.

Stage 6

Buying: suppliers, quotes, orders, payments

  1. Suppliers. Open Suppliers, press Add supplier: name, city or market, what they supply, contact, phone, WeChat, payment terms. Suppliers are shared by all your projects.
  2. Quotes. When a supplier quotes, open the item, set the supplier and the unit price, and set the status to Quoted. The supplier's line on the Suppliers page now shows items, ordered total and balance.
  3. Order. Open the supplier (inside the project) and press Purchase order PDF: their items with codes, specs, photos, prices and the balance. Send it to confirm. Set the items to Ordered: from the list, the plan or the item itself.
  4. Pay. On Payments, record each deposit or balance: supplier, date, amount in CNY, type, the bank or WeChat reference, and a photo of the receipt. The balance per supplier updates on the Overview, the Suppliers page and the purchase order. When an item is fully paid, set it to Paid.

Studio settings

What it is for: what clients, suppliers and the site team see as the sender. The studio name, phone, email and website print on every PDF, on the box labels and in the header of the share pages.

  • The default exchange rate is used for new projects. Each project keeps its own rate in Project settings.
  • The logo replaces the house glyph in the app. A square PNG or JPG looks best. The PDFs keep their own layout.
  • The app password, the database and the photo storage are set up on the server, not here.
  • Speed. Lists, the plan dots and the PDF tables show a small copy of each photo instead of the full picture, so the Items page and the plan load several times faster. Photos taken from now on get their small copy at once; for photos from before, press Make small versions under Speed until none are left. The card also shows how many pictures the server keeps in memory for the next visitor.

Projects, Overview & import

What it is for: one project per house. It holds the rooms, items, payments, boxes, images and documents of that house. Suppliers are the only thing shared between projects.

New project and Project settings

  • Client name and project name print on the schedule cover. The delivery address prints on every box label.
  • The exchange rate (CNY per 1 USD) drives every USD figure. Changing it later does not move prices, which are stored in CNY; only the USD figures change.
  • A budget in USD shows a bar on the Overview. Status (Active, On hold, Completed) is just a label on the project card.
  • A new project starts with one entry, Whole house, for items that belong to no particular room.
  • Danger zone: deleting a project removes everything in it. You have to type delete to confirm.

The Overview

  • The tiles: items (and how many have a price), total in CNY and USD against the budget, paid and balance, items and boxes received, volume packed and the container it fits, and how many items are still to buy.
  • Item status badges: tap one to list only those items.
  • Documents: every PDF and the Excel export; see PDFs & Excel.
  • By room & area (tap a room to open it on the plan; the PDF button is its checklist), By category, By supplier with ordered, paid and balance, and the items you touched most recently.

Import from Excel

  • Press Download the template on the Import page. Its Rooms sheet already lists this project's rooms and areas, so adding a floor, a room or an area is one new row: CODE - Name, the floor, and the kind (room or area). Its Shopping List sheet takes one row per item, with dropdowns for the room, category, unit, status and optional; a room you add on the Rooms sheet appears in the room dropdown at once. A How to sheet repeats these rules.
  • Several rooms in one row. The Room dropdown starts with quick picks: All rooms, All areas, All bedrooms, All bathrooms and All on Ground floor (one per floor). You can also type several rooms with ; between them ("GF-LIV - Living room; FF-BR1 - Bedroom 1"). The row becomes one item per room, each with its own code, exactly like ticking several rooms on the item form. The category stays one per item: it decides the schedule page the item prints on.
  • Updating items in Excel. Press Export all items (also Overview › Documents › Excel export): the same file, with every item filled in and its Code in the first column. Change prices, quantities, statuses, suppliers, specs or the room, then import the file. A row whose code matches an existing item updates that item: the cells you filled change, blank cells leave the value alone, and a single - clears a cell. The code, the photos and the dots on the plan stay; a changed room moves the item and removes its dots. Rows without a code are added as new items, so you can add and update in one file.
  • The Kinondoni list format imports too. Columns are matched by their headers (Code, Room, Category, Item, Must-have spec, Brand, Size, Finish, Qty, Unit, Price (CNY), Supplier, Lead time, Status, Optional, Notes); extra columns, such as the totals in the export, are ignored.
  • Rooms are matched by code and created when new, never duplicated. A Kind column is honoured; new rooms without one are classified by name.
  • Every item row is added as a new item; items are never merged. List only what you are adding, or tick Delete all existing items in this project first to start over. Photos on deleted items are lost.
  • Rows whose Room or Item starts with (example) are ignored. Photos are not in Excel: after importing, open each item and add the sample photo, or use Quick capture.

Rooms & areas

What it is for: the list of places in the house, each with a short code. Codes end up on items, labels and boxes, so a delivery goes straight to where it will be installed.

Rooms and areas

  • A room is a space you furnish and finish: living room, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, office. An area is a zone that still gets items but is not a room: entrance, hall and corridors, stairs, landing, balconies, verandah, terrace, carport, and the whole-house entries.
  • Both work exactly the same way: codes, items, boxes, labels, a box on the plan. The difference is only how they are listed and counted: rooms first, then areas, and "11 rooms · 8 areas" instead of "19 rooms".
  • Kind on Auto decides by the name. Sort rooms & areas by name does it for every entry at once; switch any entry back by hand.

Adding and editing

  • Code: short and unique, upper-case, dashes instead of spaces. GF for ground floor, FF for first floor is a good habit (GF-KIT, FF-MBR). Codes are never renumbered, because they are printed on labels and purchase orders.
  • Floor: the floor name must match the one you give the floor plan, so the plan and the rooms find each other. "Ground" and "Ground floor" count as the same.
  • Floor area and wall tile area in m² are optional and help you work out tile quantities. Notes are for the installers (finish level, access).
  • Open an entry to edit it: change the code, name, floor, kind or order and press Save. From there: Items, Checklist PDF, Labels, On the plan.
  • Deleting an entry keeps its items and boxes; they lose their room and show under "Not in a room or area" until you file them again.

Floor plans & PDF pages

What it is for: the architect's drawings, kept with the project. They print in the client schedule and the room checklists, and they power the interactive plan. The same page holds the cover picture and the mood board of the client schedule.

Adding plans

  • JPG or PNG pictures are added straight away; give the floor, and optionally the sheet reference and a caption, before you upload.
  • A PDF drawing set goes through the page picker (next). You can upload a revised set later: pages already added stay even if you delete the old PDF.
  • Plans are kept at a higher resolution than photos so room names stay readable when you zoom.
  • Drawings are a reference only: rooms and items are never created from them. You decide the scope.

The page picker

  • At upload, the app reads the title block of every page: the drawing title ("Ground Floor Plan (Furniture Layout)"), the sheet number after "Drawing No" (A-101) and the floor the title names.
  • Pages titled floor / roof / site plan are ticked, with floor, sheet and caption filled in. Elevations, sections, services drawings, notes that refer to a plan and cover sheets with a drawing list are left unticked.
  • Check, correct, untick, and press Add ticked pages. Nothing is added until you do.
  • A scanned PDF has no text to read, so nothing is prefilled: tick the plan pages and type the floor yourself.

Tagging, and several sheets for one floor

  • Every plan carries a floor, a sheet reference, a caption and what it shows; change them under the picture and press Save. The caption prints with the plan ("Ground floor · A-101 · Furniture Layout").
  • Which plan is the main plan? The sheet set to Main plan (general floor plan): the architect's floor plan of that floor, with or without furniture drawn on it. Under every plan the page says Main plan of its floor or Layer of it. With two general plans on one floor (a dimension plan and a setting-out plan, say), the one that carries room boxes is the main plan. A floor that only has a furniture layout uses it as the main plan until a general plan arrives.
  • Plans from before layers existed were filed as separate floors ("Ground Electrical"). Such a plan shows a yellow note: press Make it a layer of Ground floor and it moves under that floor as its electrical sheet, keeping any boxes it already has. Or set the floor and Shows by hand and press Save.
  • What a sheet shows: Main plan, Furniture layout, Electrical & lighting, Plumbing & sanitary, Ceiling, or Flooring & tiles. The main plan is the general floor plan of the floor: it carries the room boxes, prints in the schedule overview and is where rooms are marked. Every other sheet of the same floor is a layer of it: it appears under a Sheet switch on the Plan page, borrows the room boxes, and holds its own item dots (sofas on the furniture layout, pendants on the electrical sheet). The page picker reads this from the title block ("Ground Floor Plan (Furniture Layout)" becomes the furniture layer of Ground floor, "Electrical Layout Plan" its electrical layer).
  • A plan without a floor prints up front in the schedule and does not join a floor's tab on the Plan page.
  • "n rooms marked · mark rooms" under a plan jumps to mark mode for that plan.

The interactive plan

What it is for: browsing and updating the house room by room, straight on the drawing. Tap a room and its items appear; tap a dot and you see which item sits there. It is also what the client sees on their link.

Mark the rooms (once per plan)

  1. Open Plan, choose the floor tab, press Mark rooms.
  2. Pick a room in the list (rooms first, then areas; rooms of other floors are folded below), then drag a box over it on the drawing. It saves at once and the list moves to the next unplaced one.
  3. Drag a box to move it, pull its corner to resize it, press × in the list to remove it. The pill above the drawing counts "n of m placed". Press Done marking when you finish.

Use it every day

  • Tap a room. Its panel opens beside the plan (as a sheet from the bottom on a phone): items, total and how many have arrived, then the items by category with a status dropdown each.
  • Act on the spot. Change a status, press Add item or Capture for that room, List to open it in the item list, PDF for its checklist, or open an item. Saving brings you back to the plan. The room's own details (code, name, floor, kind, notes) are in the fold at the bottom of the panel.
  • Place an item. Tap the pin on an item, then the spot on the drawing where it goes. The dot shows the item's photo and its status colour. Drag a dot to move it, tap the pin again to find it, press × twice to remove it. An item that moves to another room loses its dot so you can place it again.
  • Read the drawing. Use − / Fit / +, pinch on a phone, or double-tap. Items hides and shows the dots. Full size opens the drawing on its own.
  • Switch sheets. A floor with more than one sheet shows a Sheet switch under the floor tabs: Main plan, Furniture layout, Electrical & lighting, Plumbing & sanitary… The room boxes are the main plan's, shown on every sheet, so tapping a room works everywhere. Dots are placed per sheet: put a pendant's dot on the electrical sheet while you look at it, a sofa's on the furniture layout. The small number on a sheet is its dot count.
  • Marking on a layer. Press Mark rooms on a furniture, electrical or plumbing sheet and it offers to copy the main plan's boxes to that sheet; do that only when the sheet is framed differently and the borrowed boxes sit off. From then on the sheet keeps its own boxes.
  • Each box shows the room code and name, a count of its items and a thin ring that fills as items arrive. Dashed boxes are areas.

Editing an item

  • Apply to the other rooms (ticked by default when the same product is in several rooms) copies the name, category, spec, size, finish, brand, price, unit, lead time and supplier to the other lines. Quantity, status, notes and photos always stay per room.
  • Add this item to more rooms copies it into the ticked rooms, each with its own code and the status To buy. The same chips are there: all bedrooms, all bathrooms, a floor.
  • Photos: delete, or Make cover. With Claude turned on, Fill in with Claude fills the empty fields from the cover photo.
  • Duplicate makes a copy to tweak. Delete item removes the item and its photos; its code is not reused.
  • Plan / On the plan at the top opens the room on the drawing, with the item highlighted if it has a dot.

Prices & currency

Prices are kept in CNY, because that is what you pay. Every total, PDF and export uses the CNY price, and the USD figures come from the project's exchange rate.

  1. When you type a unit price, choose CNY or USD next to it. The line below shows the conversion as you type.
  2. A price typed in USD is stored in CNY at the project's rate, and the app remembers that you typed it in USD so the form shows the same number next time.
  3. The currency you pick is remembered on your phone for the next new item.
{{ shot('item-price', 'The price row: the amount, the currency it was typed in, and the conversion below.', 'narrow') }}

To change the exchange rate, open Project settings. Prices already stored in CNY do not move; only the USD figures change. The client always sees USD.

Quick capture & drafts

What it is for: shooting samples fast, without typing. Each photo becomes a draft you complete later, with good Wi-Fi.

  1. Press Quick capture (the camera button, also in the middle of the bottom bar on a phone). Choose the room and category if you know them, then Take photo. Every photo is saved straight away as a draft and the counter goes up.
  2. Later, open Drafts. Give each draft a name, room, quantity and price, and press Save as item. It gets a code and starts counting. All fields… opens the full form.
  3. Discard deletes a draft and its photo.

Drafts never appear in totals, PDFs, Excel or the client link until they have a name. The Drafts badge in the menu shows how many are waiting.

Saving from the web

What it is for: turning a supplier page, a Taobao or 1688 listing or a Pinterest pin into an item or a mood-board picture, with the picture stored in the project.

  1. Paste the link in the web link field of an item or of the mood board. The main picture of the page is downloaded and stored when you save.
  2. Or set up the Save from web button once (Studio menu): drag Save to HBA to the bookmarks bar, or add the iPhone shortcut as the page explains. From then on, on any website, press it (or Share → Save to HBA) and the page lands here with the picture ready to save as a new item, a mood-board image, a cover or a floor plan.
  3. With Claude turned on, a new item saved this way is filled in from the page: name, brand, size, finish and a CNY price if the page shows one. Check it on Drafts, then save.

Some sites block downloads. If so, save the picture to your phone and upload it the normal way.

Suppliers

What it is for: who sells what, how to reach them, and, inside a project, what has been ordered and paid and how they pack.

  • Add supplier: name, city or market, what they supply, contact person, phone, WeChat, email, payment terms, notes. The supplier book is shared by all your projects.
  • Pick the supplier on each item. Inside a project the supplier's line shows items, ordered total, paid and balance, and whether a packing link is active.
  • Open a supplier inside a project for the purchase order PDF, the money and the packing link (see purchasing and packing). Opened from the Studio menu, without a project, it shows the supplier's items across all projects.
  • Deleting a supplier keeps the items; they just lose the supplier.

Purchasing: quotes, orders, payments

What it is for: following every item from "to buy" to "paid", and knowing at any moment what you owe each supplier.

The cycle

  1. To buy: the item is on the schedule, no supplier or price yet.
  2. Quoted: a supplier gave a price. Set the supplier and the unit price on the item; set the status to Quoted. The ordered total of that supplier now includes it.
  3. Ordered: you confirmed. Send the purchase order PDF from the supplier's page (items, must-haves, room codes, prices, total, paid and balance, and a note asking them to mark every carton with the room code).
  4. Paid: fully paid. Record the payments as you make them (next).

Payments

  • On Payments, record each payment: supplier, date, amount in CNY, type (Deposit, Balance, Full payment, Other), the bank or WeChat reference, a note, and a photo of the receipt.
  • Ordered = the total of that supplier's items in this project. Paid = the payments recorded. Balance = ordered minus paid. These show on the Overview, on the Suppliers page, on the supplier's page and on the purchase order.
  • The tile Balance to pay on the Payments page is the whole project. Delete a payment with the bin icon if you recorded it wrongly.

Packing & boxes

What it is for: making sure every box reaches the right room and nothing is missing on site.

  • Add a box: supplier, the room it is installed in, what is inside, the item codes, qty inside, size in cm, weight and notes. The app numbers boxes Box n of N per room, works out the volume and suggests a container size at the top.
  • The By column says whether you or the supplier entered the box. Suppliers enter theirs through their packing link; they can delete a carton until it is marked received.
  • Received on a box when it arrives; Undo if needed. The Overview counts boxes received.
  • Filter by supplier or room. Print box labels and Packing list PDF respect the supplier filter, so you can print one supplier's labels only.
  • The label carries the room code large, the room name and floor, "Box n of N for this room" (or area), the supplier and box number, the contents, item codes, quantity and weight, and the delivery address.

The client link

What it is for: letting the client follow progress without logging in, without you sending files back and forth.

  1. Open Project settings and press Copy link under Client link. Send it over WhatsApp or email. Preview as client shows exactly what they see.
  2. They see the totals in USD, the mood board, the floor plan with the rooms and the item dots (tap a room, see what goes in it), the items by category with photos, USD prices and delivery status, and Download PDF buttons. They cannot change anything.
  3. They never see suppliers, your notes, CNY prices, payments or drafts.
  4. If the link gets into the wrong hands, press Reset link. The old one stops working at once; send the new one.

Questions & problems

The app feels slow.

Three different things look the same. The first page after a quiet spell takes a few seconds and the next ones are quick: Replit starts the app and the database wakes up, which is normal; an always-on deployment removes it. Pages with many photos slow on a device that has not seen them yet: open Studio settings and press Make small versions until no older photo is left, so lists load small copies. Every page slow, even without photos: the database is far from the app or asleep; tell your developer.

The electrical plan shows no room boxes.

Boxes are drawn on the floor's main plan and borrowed by the other sheets of that floor. Check that both sheets carry the same floor name, and that the general floor plan is set to show "Main plan (general floor plan)" under Images & plans. An electrical sheet uploaded before layers existed usually sits under its own floor name ("Ground Electrical"): the Images & plans page shows a yellow note on it with a one-press fix. If the electrical sheet is framed differently and the borrowed boxes sit off, press Mark rooms on it and copy the boxes to adjust them there.

Can I change many prices or statuses at once?

Yes, in Excel. Import → Export all items, change the cells, import the file back. Rows are matched by their Code, so the items are updated rather than added again; blank cells leave a value alone and - clears one. Photos and plan dots stay.

I want one line per item, not one per room.

Open Items and choose Item list in the view switch at the right of the filters. The same product in several rooms is one line there, with its rooms, the total quantity and value and a count per status. Press a column heading to sort; the Table view sorts the same way.

I uploaded the plan, but the room is not listed under it on the Plan page.

The room's floor and the plan's floor must have the same name. Check the floor on the Rooms page and under the plan on Images & plans ("Ground" and "Ground floor" count as the same; "GF" does not). In mark mode, rooms of other floors are in the folded list below, so you can still place them.

The PDF pages were not ticked in the page picker.

The PDF has no readable text (a scan or a flattened export), so there was nothing to read. Tick the plan pages and type the floor and sheet yourself. Everything else works the same.

A photo upload failed.

Usually the Wi-Fi. Take the photo again; Quick capture keeps the ones that went through. Photos are shrunk before upload, so one photo at a time nearly always goes through even on a slow connection.

An item is in the wrong room.

Open the item and change Room or area, then save. Its code stays as it is (codes are never renumbered); its dot on the plan is removed so you can place it again in the new room.

I deleted a room by mistake.

Its items and boxes are kept; they show under "Not in a room or area" on the Overview and as "Unassigned" in the item filter. Add the room again and set it on each item.

Why can the client see prices?

The client link always shows USD prices, because the schedule is a priced document for the client. For the site team or a supplier, use Schedule without prices or Site schedule by floor under Documents.

The USD totals changed although I changed no price.

The project's exchange rate changed (Project settings). Prices are stored in CNY and do not move; the USD figures follow the rate.

Why is the code of a deleted item not reused?

Codes are printed on labels, purchase orders and packing lists that may already be with a supplier. A code never changes meaning once it exists.

A supplier cannot open their packing link.

Check the whole link was copied (press Copy link again). No login is needed. If the link was disabled, press Create packing link again and send the new one.

Can two people work at the same time?

Yes. There is one password and one set of data; each page shows the latest state when it loads.

A photo is missing from an item.

Open the item and upload it again. Photos are kept in the project's storage, never on your phone only.

I want to start a project over.

To replace the items, use Import with Delete all existing items in this project first ticked. To remove the whole project, open Project settings and use the danger zone. Both cannot be undone.
